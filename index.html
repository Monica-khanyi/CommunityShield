Building Climate-Resilient Communities

CommunityShield provides practical climate preparedness advice and a simple way to document local infrastructure problems.

🌧️ Flood Preparedness

  • Stay away from flooded roads and fast-moving water.
  • Follow trusted local weather and emergency updates.
  • Keep important emergency contacts accessible.

💧 Save Water

  • Turn off taps when water is not needed.
  • Repair leaking taps when it is safe to do so.
  • Follow local water-use restrictions.

☀️ Extreme Heat Safety

  • Drink water regularly.
  • Stay in shade or a cool place when possible.
  • Check on vulnerable neighbours when safe to do so.

📢 Report a Community Problem
